Mentorship and Leadership Development Hub

Guide. Equip. Empower.

Developing People. Raising Leaders. Transforming Communities.

We identify, develop and equip people to become confident, responsible and effective leaders in their families, workplaces, churches and communities. Leadership grows through learning, mentorship, experience, character, accountability and service.

Connect with this Hub Executive summary (PDF)
Mentorship & Guidance
Leadership Development
Skills & Capacity Building
Personal & Professional Growth

Our purpose

  • Identify and nurture leadership potential
  • Support personal and professional development
  • Connect people with experienced mentors
  • Build communication, teamwork and interpersonal skills
  • Encourage servant leadership, accountability and character
  • Prepare people to contribute positively to society

Our vision

A generation of confident, responsible and servant-hearted leaders who transform families, communities and society.

What we offer

How the Mentorship & Leadership Hub can help

  • One-on-one and group mentorship
  • Leadership workshops and development programmes
  • Personal development seminars
  • Leadership breakfasts and panel discussions
  • Public speaking and communication training
  • Team building and networking
  • Workplace readiness
  • Entrepreneurship and community leadership
  • Book discussions and knowledge-sharing sessions
Who we serve

Who we serve

  • Students
  • Young professionals
  • Emerging leaders
  • Church workers
  • Entrepreneurs
  • Established professionals
  • Anyone seeking growth and mentorship

Our commitment
