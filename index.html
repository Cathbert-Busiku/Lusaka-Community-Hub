Building Bridges To The Harvest (BBTTH) · Lusaka Community Hub Ministry

Building people. Strengthening communities.

Many Bridges. One Community.

The Building Bridges To The Harvest (BBTTH) Initiative is a programme of RCCG IWC that seeks to reach and support people and communities beyond the Church. Through this initiative, the Lusaka Community Hub (LCH) Ministry puts this vision into action by organising practical programmes and activities that address the needs of the community.

How it works

From first hello to the right support

  1. Reach out

    Visit the Care Room Hub, send a WhatsApp message or call the Secretariat. You don't need to belong to a church.

  2. We listen

    Someone takes time to hear your story, offers encouragement and prayer where welcomed, and helps identify what you need.

  3. We connect you

    We refer you to the right Hub, ministry, pastoral team or external service, and we follow up so you're not left alone.

Keep Lusaka Clean

Led by the Community Service Hub, Keep Lusaka Clean promotes shared responsibility for clean public spaces, good sanitation and a healthier city. Volunteers of every age join clean-ups, tree planting and school beautification days.

  • Community clean-ups and sanitation
  • Tree planting and environmental awareness
  • School beautification days
