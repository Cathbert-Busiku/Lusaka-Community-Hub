Legacy Circle Hub

People. Purpose. Lasting Impact.

Celebrating Experience. Inspiring Generations. Building Legacies.

A platform for seasoned adults, retirees and pre-retirees to stay connected, purposeful and engaged in society. We value the experience, wisdom and skills you have built over a lifetime, and the difference they can make for others.

Connect with this Hub Executive summary (PDF)
Intergenerational Connection
Legacy Planning
Wisdom & Experience Sharing
Support & Belonging

Our purpose

  • Promote purposeful living at every stage of life
  • Create opportunities for friendship and fellowship
  • Honour and preserve the wisdom of seasoned individuals
  • Encourage mentorship and intergenerational knowledge sharing
  • Enable skills transfer and continued contribution
  • Promote wellness, personal growth and spiritual encouragement
  • Encourage community service

Our vision

Seasoned individuals who remain valued, connected and purposeful, contributing to their families and communities.

What we offer

How the Legacy Circle Hub can help

  • Mentorship and intergenerational engagement
  • Retirement and life-transition conversations
  • Wellness and personal development sessions
  • Fellowship, prayer and social gatherings
  • Skills sharing and knowledge transfer
  • Community service and volunteering
  • Cultural activities and shared learning
Who we serve

Who we serve

  • Retired and pre-retired professionals
  • Business owners
  • Former public servants
  • Educators
  • Health professionals
  • Ministry leaders
  • Community elders
