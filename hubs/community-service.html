Community Service Hub

Serve. Uplift. Create Impact.

Serving Communities. Inspiring Change. Making a Difference.

We encourage practical service, volunteering and community engagement, so that people take care of their surroundings and of one another.

Collaboration for Positive Change

Our purpose

  • Promote volunteering and active citizenship
  • Encourage environmental cleanliness and sanitation
  • Support community wellbeing
  • Mobilise people’s time, skills and resources
  • Build partnerships for community impact
  • Show compassion through practical service

Our vision

Cleaner, healthier, transformed communities where people take responsibility and contribute to the wellbeing of others.

What we do

How the Community Service Hub can help

  • Community clean-ups and sanitation
  • Tree planting and environmental awareness
  • School beautification
  • Hospital visits and outreach
  • Blood donation awareness and drives
  • Community volunteering
  • Disaster response
  • Community health awareness
Our flagship initiative

Keep Lusaka Clean

Keep Lusaka Clean promotes shared responsibility for cleanliness, sanitation and the care of public spaces, for a healthier city we can all be proud of.

Who we serve

  • Residents
  • Schools
  • Youth groups
  • Universities and colleges
  • Community organisations
  • Local leaders
  • Volunteers
  • The wider public
