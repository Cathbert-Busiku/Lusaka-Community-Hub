Welcome to the Care Room

You matter. Your story matters.

And you do not have to walk through life’s challenges alone.

We recognise that everyone goes through seasons when they need someone to listen, a helping hand, guidance or simply reassurance that they are not alone.

At the Care Room, you will find a safe and caring environment where you can share your concerns without fear of judgement. We take time to understand your needs, offer initial support and help you find the appropriate assistance.

Where additional support is needed, we help connect you to the appropriate Community Hub, relevant support services, qualified professionals or other suitable sources of assistance, depending on your needs and preferences. We also coordinate referrals and follow up to help ensure you are not left wondering where to turn next.

You are not just a concern to be attended to. You are a person to be valued, heard and cared for.

Whatever brings you to us, you will be welcomed with compassion, treated with respect and supported with care.

At the Care Room, you are seen. You are heard. You matter.

Our purpose

  • Welcome people into a warm and supportive environment
  • Provide a safe and confidential space to be heardWe provide a safe, respectful and confidential environment where individuals can freely share their concerns without fear of judgement, gossip or unauthorised disclosure. Every conversation is treated with discretion, and personal information is handled with the utmost care and only shared with appropriate consent, except where disclosure is necessary to address a serious safety concern or other legal obligation.
  • Help identify needs and the support required
  • Connect people with appropriate support services
  • Make referrals to Community Hubs, relevant support services and qualified professionals
  • Encourage follow-up and lasting relationships
  • Strengthen care and communication across the ministry

Our vision

To create a welcoming and compassionate environment where every person feels seen, valued and supported; where needs are met with understanding, meaningful relationships are nurtured, and everyone is connected to appropriate support and opportunities for growth.

What we do

How the Care Room Hub can help

  • A listening ear in a safe, confidential space
  • Encouragement and initial support
  • Conversations free from judgement or pressure
  • Help identifying your needs
  • Referrals to Community Hubs, support services and qualified professionals
  • Follow-up and coordination of referrals
Who we serve

Who can approach the Care Room Hub?

  • Anyone needing guidance
  • Anyone needing encouragement
  • Anyone looking for support services
  • People of every background
  • People of any faith or belief, or none

The Care Room Hub is open to everyone in the wider community, whatever your background, beliefs or religious affiliation. You can receive support without being judged or pressured to embrace a particular faith.
