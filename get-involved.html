Partner with us

Four ways to partner with the LCH Ministry

Whether you give funds, time, expertise or institutional support, every partnership helps the seven Hubs reach more people.

Financial Partners

Individuals or organisations providing financial support.

Why it matters
Funding keeps Hub programmes running: venues, learning materials, outreach supplies, transport and clean-up equipment.
Ways to contribute
  • One-off contributions
  • Monthly contributions
  • Annual contributions
  • Sponsorship of a specific Hub or event
How to take part
Choose an amount and how often you would like to give. The Secretariat confirms your pledge and sends secure payment details.
Become a Financial Partner

Mentorship Partners

Individuals offering professional guidance, mentorship and skills development.

Why it matters
Experienced people help students, young professionals and emerging leaders grow faster and with fewer missteps.
Ways to contribute
  • One-on-one or group mentoring
  • Career talks and panels
  • Skills workshops
  • Leadership breakfasts
How to take part
Tell us your field and availability. We match you with the right Hub, most often Student Success or Mentorship and Leadership.
Become a Mentorship Partner

Technical and Resource Partners

Organisations or individuals providing technical expertise, equipment or other resources.

Why it matters
Equipment, venues, software, printing and professional services stretch what each Hub can deliver.
Ways to contribute
  • Venues and meeting space
  • Equipment and supplies
  • Printing, media and IT support
  • Professional services
How to take part
Describe what you can provide and for how long. The Secretariat agrees the details and how your support will be acknowledged.
Become a Technical and Resource Partner

Strategic and Institutional Partners

Organisations collaborating with LCH to support programme implementation and community impact.

Why it matters
Schools, councils, businesses, NGOs and churches working alongside LCH reach more people and sustain results.
Ways to contribute
  • Joint programmes and outreach
  • Referral partnerships
  • Shared community projects
  • Research and impact reporting
How to take part
Register your organisation's interest. We arrange a meeting to agree shared goals and a simple partnership agreement.
Become a Strategic and Institutional Partner
Financial partnership

Give in the way that suits you

Choose a one-off, monthly or annual contribution. You can support the ministry's general work or a specific Hub.

One-off

A single gift towards a Hub, an event or the ministry's general work.

Monthly

Regular support that lets the Hubs plan programmes with confidence.

Annual

A yearly commitment, ideal for organisations and sponsorship budgets.

How giving works

  1. Make a pledgeChoose an amount, how often and how you prefer to give.
  2. We confirmThe Secretariat confirms your pledge and the official account details.
  3. Give securelyPay by mobile money, bank transfer into official ministry accounts only.
  4. Receive an acknowledgementWe thank you and keep you updated on the impact of your gift.
