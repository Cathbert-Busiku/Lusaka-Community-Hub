Student Success Hub

Learn, Grow, Achieve.

Empowering Students to Learn, Grow and Succeed

We support learners through their academic journey and into their careers. Success includes good grades and much more: personal growth, character, leadership, relationships, emotional wellbeing and readiness for work.

Connect with this Hub Executive summary (PDF)
Academic Support
Study Skills & Resources

Our purpose

  • Support academic and personal development
  • Build strong study habits and skills
  • Offer mentorship and guidance
  • Teach practical life skills
  • Develop leadership, character and responsibility
  • Prepare students for further education and work
  • Encourage teamwork and communication

Our vision

Students who are confident, well-rounded and purpose-driven, and who contribute to their families, workplaces and communities.

What we offer

How the Student Success Hub can help

  • Academic guidance and study support
  • Mentorship
  • Career guidance and readiness
  • Leadership and character development
  • Financial literacy and entrepreneurship awareness
  • Communication and problem-solving skills
  • Digital literacy and employability skills
  • Christian values and responsible living
Who we serve

Who we serve

  • School learners
  • University and college students
  • Recent graduates
  • Young people preparing for further education or work
